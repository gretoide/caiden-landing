About the artist

Good day!
I'm Caiden Mikael Dri.

A passionate artist who has dedicated his life to expanding his knowledge within the artistic world. Creativity and consistency are the pillars of the work.

With over ten years of experience in prop making, I have mastered a wide range of techniques that have established me as one of the most recognized professionals in my field in Argentina, working on ad campaigns for multiple renowned brands and franchises.

In my last year before Australia I was honored as the Argentinian Cosplay Champion, served as an International Judge at the Yamato Cosplay Cup, and my work was acknowledged at the Martín Fierro Awards, Argentina's equivalent of the Oscars.

Today I'm based in Perth, ready to bring your dream projects to life, with every piece durable and visually stunning for any production environment.
